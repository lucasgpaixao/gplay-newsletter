Back when Rockstar showed off a bunch of GTA 6 gameplay on Netflix, one thing was incredibly clear – the game was running around 30 fps. And now that Rockstar has confirmed that it is targeting 30 fps on every console, including the PS5 Pro, it's clear that the game likely never would have run at a solid 60 on console.
