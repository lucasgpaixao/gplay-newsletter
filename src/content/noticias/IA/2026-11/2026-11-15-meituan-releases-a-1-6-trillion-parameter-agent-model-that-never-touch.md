Meituan has released the weights for LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-trillion-parameter model built for AI agents and trained entirely on Chinese hardware. On Terminal Bench, it scores 88.4 points, landing just behind Claude Opus 5. The sample deployment runs on 194 Huawei Ascend chips, and Meituan makes no mention of Nvidia GPUs....
